# 🎓 Undergraduate Track: Advanced Astrobotany Research

Welcome to the **Undergraduate Track** of the AstroBotany Investigation Resource (AIR). This track is designed for upper-level undergraduate students, advanced research teams, and educators wishing to integrate cutting-edge space biology, hardware engineering, data science, and computational modeling into their curricula.

---

## 🎯 Program Objectives & Core Competencies

By participating in this track, students will develop competencies in the following core areas:

1. **Experimental Design & Execution**:
   - Formulating hypotheses regarding plant behavior in simulated microgravity.
   - Preparing sterile culture systems (agar plates, tissue culture) for continuous phenotyping.
   - Utilizing professional simulated-microgravity hardware (clinostats and Random Positioning Machines).

2. **Advanced Phenotypic Analysis & Data Science**:
   - Building and calibrating specialized scientific photography platforms.
   - Processing image datasets using Python-based pipelines, Fiji/ImageJ, and **PlantCV** to extract quantitative parameters such as Green Area Index (GAI) and Root System Architecture (RSA).

3. **Computational Biology & Systems Modeling**:
   - Simulating auxin-transport dynamics at a multicellular scale to predict gravitropic curvature.
   - Quantitative modeling of root hydraulic conductance, hydropatterning, and metabolic pathways.

4. **Omics & Bioinformatics**:
   - Mining NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR) / GeneLab to analyze real spaceflight RNA-seq datasets.
   - Correlating gene expression networks with observed macroscopic phenotypic changes in space environments.

---

## 🛠️ Required Equipment & Materials

To fully implement the 12-week schedule, the following resources are recommended:

* **Hardware & Growth Platforms**:
  * Simulated microgravity platform (e.g., CoSE SciSpinner Max RPM or custom 2D clinostats).
  * Sterile laminar flow hood or clean-air work bench.
  * Plant growth LED chambers with programmable spectrum and photoperiod control.
* **Consumables**:
  * Microgreen seeds (e.g., Mustard, Mizuna, Chia, Radish).
  * High-purity agar-agar or plant-based gellan gum.
  * Murashige & Skoog (MS) basalt salt mixture and plant hormones (e.g., Auxin: 2,4-D or IAA).
  * Petri dishes (100mm x 15mm) and parafilm.
* **Software & Computing**:
  * Personal computers or cloud environments (e.g., Google Colab) with Python 3, Fiji/ImageJ, and R.
  * Internet access for database mining (GeneLab, Epicollect5).
